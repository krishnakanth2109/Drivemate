Privacy Policy for Drivemate

Application: Drivemate (com.drivemate.live)
Effective Date: November 09, 2026
Last Updated: November 09, 2026

Drivemate is committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and what rights you have in relation to it when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us, as well as device information and telemetry necessary to provide vehicle management and driver community services.

A. Personal Information You Provide

  • Account Credentials: Full name, email address, profile photo, and authentication identifiers provided through Firebase Authentication (including Google Sign-In or email/password).
  • Vehicle & Driver Details: Make, model, year, license plate (optional), and maintenance records you voluntarily log into "My Garage".
  • User-Generated Content: Ride postings, routes, messages, photos, and comments you share within the driver community or live messaging tab.

B. Device & Permission-Based Data

To provide telematics, vehicle assistance, and messaging features, Drivemate requests access to specific device permissions:

Permission Type Purpose
ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION
ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION		 Precise & Approximate Location Used to display your current position on maps, calculate routes, provide speedometer readings, and locate nearby drivers or ride-share posts while using the app. Location data is never sold or shared with advertisers.
CAMERA Hardware Camera Allows you to capture photos for your driver profile, vehicle documents, and community ride posts. Access is only active when you explicitly open the camera.
READ_MEDIA_IMAGES
READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE		 Photo Library Allows you to select existing photos from your device library to upload profile avatars and vehicle pictures.
RECORD_AUDIO
MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS		 Microphone & Audio Used solely when recording voice messages or participating in audio communication within the driver messaging channels.
INTERNET
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE		 Network Access Enables secure HTTPS communication with cloud servers, real-time socket messaging, and cloud authentication.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected data strictly for operational and service delivery purposes, including:

  • Operating and maintaining the Drivemate platform and user accounts.
  • Facilitating real-time driver-to-driver communications via WebSocket channels.
  • Calculating navigation paths, distance units (Miles/Kilometers), and speedometer metrics.
  • Authenticating users securely and preventing unauthorized access or abuse.
  • Delivering important service alerts, security notifications, and community replies.

3. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We only share information with trusted third-party infrastructure providers necessary to power the app:

  • Google Firebase (Google LLC): Cloud authentication, secure user tokens, crash analytics, and cloud performance monitoring. Google Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudinary: End-to-end encrypted cloud storage and Content Delivery Network (CDN) for user avatars and vehicle media. Cloudinary Privacy Policy.
  • MongoDB Atlas: Enterprise-grade encrypted database hosting for vehicle profiles, community postings, and message history. MongoDB Privacy Policy.
  • Render Cloud Services: Secure API server hosting with SSL/TLS encryption.

4. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain your personal data only as long as necessary to fulfill the services outlined in this Privacy Policy, or until you request its deletion.

How to Delete Your Account and Data:
1. In-App Immediate Deletion: In the Drivemate app, navigate to Profile → Settings → Danger Zone → Delete Account. Confirming deletion will permanently erase your profile, credentials, vehicles, community posts, and chat history from our databases immediately.

2. By Email Request: You can contact us at krishnakanth2109@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request". All associated data will be permanently wiped within 30 days.

5. Security of Your Information

We employ modern administrative and technical safeguards to protect your personal information, including 256-bit SSL/TLS encryption for all data in transit, token-based API authentication, and encrypted databases at rest. However, no internet transmission is 100% immune to vulnerabilities, so we recommend maintaining a secure device passcode.

6. Children's Privacy

Drivemate is not directed to individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we immediately delete such data from our servers.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
